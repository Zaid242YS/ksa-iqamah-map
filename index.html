What this app is designed to do

Moving a household is a business and family decision, not simply a visa transaction. This planner turns a broad aspiration into a selected route, documented gaps, an estimated schedule and a cash reserve target. It helps a professional evaluate an offer, a founder distinguish business registration from residency, and a family see what must happen before relocation.

The Creed to Commerce approach used here connects intention and integrity with evidence, contractual responsibility, useful work and stewardship of family resources. These are coaching prompts developed for this app, not a religious ruling or an official Saudi assessment. The app does not score faith, nationality or ethnicity.

No student route is included. Tourist, Umrah and business-visit permissions must not be treated as authorization to take employment. A residency card does not replace professional licensing or business permissions.

Renewal and contingency discipline

90 / 60 / 30-day review

As a personal planning practice, review expiry dates 90 days ahead; request employer or adviser confirmation 60 days ahead; check completed action 30 days ahead. These reminders are not statutory deadlines.

If employment or funding ends

Contact the employer, Qiwa or the Premium Residency Center immediately about the route-specific transfer, cancellation or departure position. Do not assume a universal grace period. Maintain emergency funds, document custody and a lawful exit plan.

Family affordability & runway

All amounts are SAR. Enter household out-of-pocket amounts after confirmed employer benefits. Investment capital is separate from spendable family savings.

Keep responsibility clear. Under Labor Law Article 40, recruitment, employee Iqamah/work-permit issuance and renewal, and specified employment-related costs belong to the employer. Do not silently add them to household costs. Verify dependent charges and benefits separately in writing.

Relocate in stages

A sensible option is to establish the principal applicant’s lawful status, housing and insurance first, then complete family residence. Treat family visits as visits, not substitutes for residence.

Protect family continuity

Plan school admission and language support, emergency travel, elder care, spouse career options and access to documents. Ask how status changes, death, separation or business closure affect each family member.

What does the app not do?

It does not submit applications, validate documents, book appointments, provide live case status, guarantee eligibility, calculate taxes or make binding legal determinations. Its readiness percentage measures checklist completion only. There is no automatic rule-update feed.

How should I verify a job or intermediary?

Confirm the employer’s legal identity, written contract, actual occupation, salary, benefits and authorization through the relevant official systems. Never buy a fictitious job or pay for an alleged guaranteed permanent Iqamah. Request an itemized scope and receipt from any authorized adviser.

What should a business founder ask?

Ask MISA and the Saudi Business Center which registration, activity, ownership and sector approvals apply to the proposed entity. Establish the company’s immigration and employment obligations separately. Ask about ZATCA registration, accounting, payroll, social insurance, localization, municipal permissions and regulated operations. Incorporation costs, investment capital and household runway must be separately funded.
